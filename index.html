Changyue Liao

I received my M.S. from Zhejiang University. I am interested in language models, machine learning, and efficient AI systems.

Blog  /  Github  /  Google Scholar  /  LinkedIn

Research

My interests broadly lie in machine learning and AI systems. I care about how language models reason, learn from data, and run reliably and efficiently in real-world systems.

Language Models and Reasoning

How can models understand language, represent knowledge, and solve problems that require multi-step reasoning?

Efficient AI Systems

How can we make model training and inference faster, more reliable, and easier to use?
